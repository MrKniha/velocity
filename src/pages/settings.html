Import and default browser

Bring your history and favourite sites over from another browser, and open links with Velocity.

Default browser

Open links with VelocityWindows lets you choose the default browser only in its own Settings. This opens that page: pick Velocity for web links. Needs Velocity installed with its setup.

Import

Bookmarks-bar sites become Essentials (up to 12). Cookies keep you logged in to sites and can only be brought over from Firefox: Chrome, Edge, Brave and other Chromium browsers lock their cookies and passwords so that only they can read them. If a browser is open, close it first.
